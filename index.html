    Journal

    More Than a Property. A Way of Life.

    A house on the Côte d'Azur is a long lunch on a terrace that faces the water. A Tuscan estate is your own wine in the cellar by the third autumn. A Milan penthouse is the walk home past La Scala after the opera.

    We sell homes, but our clients buy the days they will spend in them. That is why every listing we take comes with the details we would want to know: where the light falls at six, which café opens first, how long the drive is to the ski lifts.
